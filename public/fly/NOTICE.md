# FlyWire + mushroom body

Soma coordinates and cell types: FlyWire Codex FAFB v783
(Dorkenwald et al., Nature 634, 124–138, 2024). License: CC BY-NC 4.0.
https://flywire.ai/ · https://codex.flywire.ai/

Mushroom-body rate model (k-winners-take-all Kenyon cells, dopamine-gated
depression of KC→MBON synapses): ported from WilliamJones/fly-blackjack
`flybrain/mb/model.py` (MIT License).
https://github.com/WilliamJones/fly-blackjack

FaceNet 512-d embeddings are an authored mapping onto antennal-lobe
projection neurons (the “odour”). That mapping is ours, not biology.
